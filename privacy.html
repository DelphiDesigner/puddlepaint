Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Puddle Paint is a painting app for children. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Puddle Paint has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use any third-party code that collects data.

What stays on your device

Pictures, settings and sound choices are saved on your device only. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Sharing pictures

A grown-up can share pictures from the grown-ups area with Apple's share sheet, for example to Messages, Mail or Photos. The picture goes only where the grown-up chooses. The shared file holds the picture only: no name, date or location.

Tips

Optional tips ("Buy me a coffee") are handled by Apple through In-App Purchase. We do not receive your name or payment details.

Statistics from Apple

If you agreed to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous numbers, such as downloads and crash reports. We cannot see who you are.

Children

Because we collect no personal information, we collect none from children. This meets the rules of the App Store Kids Category, COPPA and GDPR.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
