Common questions

How do I open the grown-ups area?
Hold the lock button at the top of the tool column. The grown-ups area has settings, sounds, sharing and tips.

How do I save a picture to Photos or send it to family?
In the grown-ups area, open "Share pictures", choose pictures, and tap Share.

How do I turn the sounds down or off?
In the grown-ups area, under Sounds. The device's silent switch also mutes the app.

My child is left-handed.
In the grown-ups area, turn on "Colours on the right".

Where are the pictures?
The camera button saves the picture to the gallery. The gallery button shows all saved pictures.
