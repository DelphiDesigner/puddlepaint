Paint that really mixes.

A calm painting app for children aged 3 to 7. Brush blue over yellow and watch it turn green.

Real colour mixing

Paint mixes on the paper like real paint, so children learn colours by trying.

Pip's puddle

Drop colours into Pip, a little paint puddle, and paint with the mix.

Brushes to explore

Soft paint, a grainy crayon, sparkly glitter and an eraser, in three sizes.

Pictures to colour

21 starter pictures and a fill bucket: animals, a rocket, a rainbow and more.

Made for small hands

  • Big buttons and no reading needed
  • No wrong answers, no scores, no timers
  • Soft sounds that grown-ups can turn down or off
  • Colours and tools can swap sides for left-handed children
  • Pictures save on their own

No ads. No tracking. No data collected.

Puddle Paint has no ads, no accounts and no analytics, and it works without the internet. Pictures stay on your device until a grown-up chooses to share them. Read the privacy policy.
